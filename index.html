Pokemon Parallel Emerald (v1.2) AI document

Parallel Emerald is made by Joggel.

This document is intended to help you understand what the AI will do on a given turn. Everything here comes from the game's own code, and the main rules were tested by running the game's AI in real battles. The rules were then checked against Joggel's own AI source code, and they match. It works the same on every difficulty.

Brief summary about Parallel Emerald AI:

The AI gives each of its moves a score and clicks the highest one. If moves tie, it picks randomly between them. Every move starts at the same score, so in this doc scores are just written as a plus or minus. Scores from different sections add together.

The AI knows all 4 moves of the Pokemon you have out, plus its stats, HP and status. It does not know your ability or item until they have been revealed in battle.

When the AI works out damage, it always uses the highest damage roll. So if a move CAN kill on a high roll, the AI counts it as a kill. It also adds in the small chance of a critical hit: about +2% damage on a normal move, and about +6% on a high crit move like Slash or Stone Edge.

Speed ties are a coin flip each time the AI checks.

Lastly, the AI has a basic check for useless moves. If Stealth Rock is already up, it won't click it again. If you are already paralyzed, it won't try to paralyze you. These are almost all obvious, so they are at the very bottom in Extra details.

Common scores to be aware of:

There is no max score. Every move starts at 0, the AI adds up its rules, and it clicks the highest total. A score only matters compared to the AI's other moves.

Switch AI:

The AI in this game likes to hard switch. Joggel made it this way on purpose. Some of it is random, so you can't always predict it.

The AI can't hard switch if it is trapped, rooted with Ingrain, or has no Pokemon that can come in.
Every hard switch also needs a back Pokemon with a score of +1 or higher (see "Hard switch scores").
If nobody has one, the AI stays in and uses a move. Only the Absorb switch skips this.

Hard switch:

If the AI sees a bad matchup (you can KO it, it can't KO you, and you are faster), and its HP is 25% or more:
It will hard switch 100% of the time
Note: A speed tie doesn't count as you being faster.
If all 4 of its moves score +1 or lower, and its HP is 25% or more:
It will hard switch 100% of the time, unless it already switched on both of the last 2 turns
Note: A resisted best attack counts as this (+2 for highest, -2 for resisted).
If the AI is infatuated:
It will hard switch 100% of the time, unless it already picked a move that KOs you and it is faster
If the AI is drowsy from Yawn, and its HP is above 33%:
It will hard switch 100% of the time, unless it already picked a move that KOs you and it is faster
It also stays in if it knows Sleep Talk, or if it is on the ground and knows Misty Terrain or Electric Terrain.
If the AI's Perish count is about to hit 0:
It will hard switch 100% of the time, unless it has Soundproof
If the AI has Natural Cure and is asleep, frozen or badly poisoned:
It will hard switch 100% of the time
If the AI has been badly poisoned for 2+ turns, and its HP is 33% or more:
It will hard switch 50% of the time (10% if it has a raised stat)
If the AI is Cursed:
It will hard switch 50% of the time (10% if it has a raised stat)
If the AI is in Nightmare:
It will hard switch 33% of the time (7% if it has a raised stat)
If the AI is Leech Seeded:
It will hard switch 25% of the time (5% if it has a raised stat)
Note: It won't do the Toxic, Curse, Nightmare or Leech Seed switches if it has Magic Guard,
or if it already picked a move that KOs you and it is faster.
If the AI has Regenerator, no status, and its HP is 67% or lower:
It will hard switch 50% of the time (10% if it has a raised stat)
If the AI has Natural Cure, any other status, and its HP is 50% or more:
It will hard switch 25% of the time (5% if it has a raised stat)
If the last move you landed on it was a damaging Fire, Water, Electric or Grass move, and a back Pokemon absorbs it:
It will hard switch to that Pokemon 50% of the time
If the AI has a super effective move on you, it will only do this 17% of the time
Fire: Flash Fire. Water: Water Absorb, Storm Drain, Dry Skin.
Electric: Volt Absorb, Motor Drive, Lightning Rod. Grass: Sap Sipper.
If the AI has a bad matchup or only bad moves, is faster, and knows U-turn, Volt Switch or Flip Turn:
It will click that move instead of hard switching

Hard switch scores:

When the AI hard switches, it scores each back Pokemon and sends in the highest score.
It needs a score of +1 or higher. Ties go to the first one in party order.
+8Faster, OHKOs you, doesn't get 3HKO'd
+7Faster, OHKOs you, doesn't get 2HKO'd
+6Faster, 2HKOs you, doesn't get 3HKO'd
+5Slower, OHKOs you, doesn't get 3HKO'd
+4Faster, OHKOs you, doesn't get OHKO'd
+3Slower, OHKOs you, doesn't get 2HKO'd
+2Slower, 2HKOs you, doesn't get 3HKO'd
+1Slower, 3HKOs you, doesn't get 3HKO'd
0Anything else. Can't come in.
Scores below overwrite the ones above:
-1Slower and gets 2HKO'd. Can't come in.
-1Gets OHKO'd by a move you are unlikely to click (50%, no score change other 50%)
-2Gets OHKO'd by your strongest move or another likely move. Can't come in.
Note: A speed tie counts as slower here.
Baton Pass comes before these scores:
If a back Pokemon knows Baton Pass and exactly 2 Pokemon can come in, it always picks the Baton Pass user.
If 3 or more can come in, it picks the Baton Pass user 33% of the time.

Post-KO switch scores:

When a Pokemon faints, the AI scores each back Pokemon and sends in the highest score.
Ties go to the first one in party order.
+6Faster, OHKOs you, doesn't get OHKO'd
+5Faster, OHKOs you
+4Slower, OHKOs you, doesn't get OHKO'd
+3Faster, and wins the damage race
+2Slower, and still wins the damage race after taking a hit first
+1Faster
0Anything else
-1Slower and gets OHKO'd
If every back Pokemon scores 0 or lower, the AI sends out the LAST Pokemon still alive in party order.
Note: Winning the damage race means its best move does a bigger % of your HP than your best move does of its HP.
Baton Pass comes before these scores, same as hard switching.

Doubles:

The bad matchup and only bad moves switches don't exist in Doubles. Switching in Doubles is rare.

Exact setup and attack details:

These moves can never be the highest damaging move, and get -1 if they don't kill:
Blast Burn, Draco Meteor, Dragon Energy, Dream Eater, Eruption, Eternabeam, Explosion, Fleur Cannon, Focus Punch, Frenzy Plant, Giga Impact, Hydro Cannon, Hyper Beam, Leaf Storm, Meteor Assault, Mind Blown, Misty Explosion, Overheat, Prismatic Laser, Psycho Boost, Roar of Time, Rock Wrecker, Self-Destruct, Skull Bash, Solar Beam, Solar Blade, Spit Up, Superpower, Water Spout
Damage over 120% of your HP counts as 120%. If two moves tie, the one without recoil or with a side effect is the highest damaging move.
"A lot less" means more than 30% of your HP less, or close but the highest move has a side effect and this one doesn't.
High crit move that is super effective: Additional +1 (50%)
AI burned, physical moves except Facade: -2
Moxie, Beast Boost, Chilling Neigh, Grim Neigh, faster and the move kills: Additional +8
Your priority moves only make the AI think you are faster if they could KO it in 2 hits.
Speed drop attacks (Icy Wind etc): AI faster -2. AI slower +1 (73%), plus +2 if it would work.
Curse on a Ghost: trapped +3, otherwise +1, HP 50% or lower -6. Non-Ghost Curse uses General setup.
Coil gets General setup only. Hone Claws: Attack, plus +2 if it has a move under 80% accuracy. Work Up, Growth: Attack, or Sp. Atk with no physical move. Shell Smash with White Herb +1.
Cosmic Power, Stockpile: Defense and Sp. Def like Multi-stat setup. Stockpile with Swallow or Spit Up +2. Charge: Sp. Def, +2 with an Electric attack.
Double Team, Minimize: HP above 90% +3 (61%). You poisoned, AI HP 50%+ +3 (69%). You seeded or Cursed +3 (73%). AI has Ingrain +2 (50%).
Offensive setup: all attacks of that kind immune -8. Defensive setup: Barrier, Cotton Guard and similar work the same.

Exact recovery details:

The heal is compared to your strongest hit, plus its chip damage, plus 5% of its HP. Wish compares to 2 hits. Rest compares to 3 hits if faster, 4 if slower (1 hit with Chesto, Lum or Sleep Talk).
Heal amounts: 50% for most, Morning Sun etc. 67% in sun and 33% in rain, sand or hail, Shore Up 67% in sand, Rest 100%, Swallow 25/50/100%.
AI faster, you can't KO it, HP below 60%, 67% of the time: Additional +1 (Swallow too, Shore Up +2, +3 in sand). Big Root: +1.
Rest, HP below 60%, poisoned, burned, paralyzed or knows Sleep Talk: +5 instead of +3. Rest can heal in time: +1 (+2 with Chesto, Lum, Shed Skin, Early Bird, or Hydration in rain).

Exact pivot details:

+7 cases also include: AI faster, can't KO you, you are at full HP with Focus Sash, Sturdy, Multiscale or Shadow Shield (attacks only).
"Wants to switch" only counts for attacks if the AI can 2HKO you, and only with no useful boosts if it can't.
Slower cases with no score change: you can KO it (Teleport -10). You can 2HKO it, it can KO you, it isn't dying to chip. You can't 2HKO it, it can KO you, no boosts, not dying to chip. You can't 2HKO it, it can 2HKO you. Nobody can 2HKO and no boosts. Any other slower case is -10.
-10 also if the Pokemon it would switch to has under 1/8 HP with Spikes or Stealth Rock up.
No -10 if it is AI's only attack that isn't weak, or the highest damaging move and the AI can KO you.
The 30% HP penalty is only for U-turn, Volt Switch and Flip Turn. No other Pokemon: no score change.

Exact HP rules:

Your HP 21% to 40%: not Shell Smash, Quiver Dance, Coil, Shift Gear, Work Up, Growth, Hone Claws, Victory Dance. Mist, Focus Energy and Safeguard get it too.
AI's HP above 70%: also Grudge. Overheat, Draco Meteor, Leaf Storm and similar unless the AI can KO you.
AI's HP 31% to 70%: also Explosion, Conversion, Light Screen.
AI's HP 30% or lower: Bide, Conversion, Mist, Focus Energy, two turn moves, Rage, Lock-On, Safeguard, Belly Drum, Psych Up, Mirror Coat, Solar Beam, Eruption, Tickle. Explosion and Light Screen are not on this list.

More switch details:

Wonder Guard: if you have it, the AI has no super effective move, and a back Pokemon does: 67% per move. Never in Doubles.
You are off screen (Fly, Dig, etc): the AI may switch to a Pokemon that absorbs your last move. It stays if it has a raised stat and you can't KO it,
or if it picked a move that KOs you, is slower, and you can't KO it.
Order if several are true: Wonder Guard, Perish Song, Yawn, Toxic / Curse / Nightmare / Leech Seed, Infatuation, off screen, Absorb, bad matchup and only bad moves, Natural Cure and Regenerator.
Natural Cure, burned, all attacks physical: every move drops to -10 on purpose, so it switches.
Likely moves when ranking who comes in: your strongest move on its current Pokemon, any move that KOs its current Pokemon, and other moves unless one does 25% more, another KOs, or it's resisted and under half its HP.
Truant: a Truant Pokemon can't come in while it would be in danger from your Protect or a two turn move.
Truant Pokemon that is loafing next turn, with HP 50%+, may switch out if you can exploit it.
Game bugs:
Immune or resist switch: meant to be 50% and 33% if a back Pokemon is immune to or resists your last move and has a super effective move. It reads junk data. In testing it never fired (0 of 96).
Yawn: the AI stays in for Marvel Scale, a Wiki Berry, or Nature Power. These were meant to be Natural Cure, Shed Skin, Early Bird, Chesto Berry, Lum Berry and Snore.
After a KO: if the hard switch check ran earlier that turn, a Pokemon not on the list keeps its score from that check.

More on your ability:

Until revealed, the AI treats you as having no ability for damage, and guesses randomly from your Pokemon's abilities for other checks.
Shadow Tag, Arena Trap and Magnet Pull are always known.
Taunt's Oblivious and Aroma Veil check sees your real ability.
The AI knows if you hold an item (for Knock Off, Thief, Poltergeist), but not what it does until revealed.
