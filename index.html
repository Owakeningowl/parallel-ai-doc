Pokemon Parallel Emerald (v1.2) AI document

Read directly out of the game's code. Layout based on Croven's Run and Bun AI doc. Parallel Emerald is made by Joggel.

This document is intended to help you understand what the AI will do on a given turn. It covers trainer battles on every difficulty, because the AI code is the same on all of them.

The main rules were checked by running the game's real AI in set-up battles and reading its scores out of memory. Anything that didn't match was fixed.

Brief summary about Parallel Emerald AI:

The AI gives each of its 4 moves a score and clicks the highest one. Every move starts at 100. If moves tie, it picks randomly between them.

In this doc every score is written as a change from 100. So "+2" means the move ends up at 102.

The move's score is all of its rules added together. Rules from different sections stack.

Lastly, the AI has a check for useless moves. If Stealth Rock is already up, it won't click it again. If you are already paralyzed, it won't try to paralyze you. Those are all listed at the bottom of this doc.

What the AI knows about you:

Your moves:
The AI knows all 4 moves of the Pokemon you have out, from the moment it comes in.
You do not need to have used them.
Your stats, HP, speed and status:
The AI knows all of it.
Your ability:
The AI does NOT know it until it has shown up in battle.
Until then it treats you as having no ability when it works out damage.
For other checks it guesses randomly from that Pokemon's abilities.
Shadow Tag, Arena Trap and Magnet Pull are always known.
Your item:
The AI does NOT know what it does until it has shown up in battle.
Until then it treats you as having no item when it works out damage.
It does know whether you are holding something (for Knock Off, Thief and Poltergeist).
Damage:
The AI always assumes the highest damage roll. There is no random roll.
It also adds a tiny bit extra for crit chance (about 2%).
So if a move CAN kill you on a high roll, the AI counts it as a kill.
Speed:
Speed ties are a coin flip every time the AI checks.
Your priority moves only make the AI think you are faster if that move could KO it in 2 hits.

Trainer AI types:

Every trainer uses one of these. Nearly everyone is the first type.

Standard (761 of 822 trainers):
Follows everything in this doc.
Standard + First Turn Setup (25 trainers):
Same as Standard, plus a big +10 on setup moves on turn 1 (see "First turn setup").
Includes: Roxanne, Norman, Winona, Sidney, Spenser, Tabitha (1 fight), Midori (1 fight),
Rosalie, Koharu, Cindy, Rodney, Jeffrey, Lucy, Elliot, Beth, Lorenzo, Athena, Nicholas,
Isabella, Kim & Iris, Josue, Carolina.
Setup + Status (33 trainers):
Does NOT get the "Try to KO" bonuses, so kills and super effective hits score much lower.
Gets the +10 on setup moves on turn 1, and +1 on every status move that isn't immune.
Includes: Wattson (2), Maxie (2), Tabitha (2), Brandon (2), Midori (2),
Lola, Wilton, Melissa, Takao, Larry, Ethan, Autumn, Edward, Jaylen, Andrew, Anthony, Lung,
Madeline, Carol, Dwayne, Lydia, Brice, Trent, Lenny, Sophie, Coby, Lawrence, Wyatt.
Standard + Status (2 trainers, Noland):
Standard, plus +1 on every status move that isn't immune.
Wally (1 fight):
Same as Setup + Status, but without the +2 for highest damaging move.
No trainer in this game uses items. Trainers never heal with potions.

Switch AI:

The AI in this game hard switches a lot more than most hacks. Joggel made it this way on purpose. Some of it is random, so you can't read it perfectly.

The AI can NOT hard switch if:
It is trapped (Wrap, Fire Spin, Mean Look, Block and so on).
It is rooted with Ingrain.
You have Shadow Tag, Arena Trap or Magnet Pull and they affect it.
It has no other Pokemon that can come in.
Every switch below needs a back Pokemon on the "Which Pokemon comes in" list, or the AI stays in.
Only Wonder Guard and Absorb switch skip that list.
If more than one is true, this is the order it goes in:
Wonder Guard, Perish Song, Yawn, Toxic / Curse / Nightmare / Leech Seed, Infatuation,
you being in the air or underground, Absorb, Bad matchup and Only bad moves,
Natural Cure and Regenerator.

Bad matchup:

If the AI sees ALL of these, it will hard switch 100% of the time:
You can KO it this turn.
It can NOT KO you this turn.
You are faster than it (a speed tie doesn't count).
Its HP is 25% or more.
One of its back Pokemon passes the "Which Pokemon comes in" list below.

Only bad moves:

If the AI sees ALL of these, it will hard switch 100% of the time:
All 4 of its moves score +1 or lower.
Its HP is 25% or more.
It did not switch on BOTH of the last 2 turns.
One of its back Pokemon passes the "Which Pokemon comes in" list below.
This usually happens when:
All of its attacks are immune or useless.
Its best attack is resisted and it has nothing else (+2 for highest, -2 for resisted, so +0).
It is locked into a bad move by Encore or a Choice item.
It is burned, has Natural Cure, and all of its attacks are physical
(this drops every move to -10 on purpose so it switches).

U-turn instead of a hard switch:

When Bad matchup or Only bad moves say to switch:
If the AI knows one of these moves, it is faster, and the move would do damage,
it clicks that move instead of hard switching: Flip Turn, Poisonous Graze, U-turn, Volt Switch

Wonder Guard:

If you have Wonder Guard, the AI has no super effective move, and a back Pokemon does:
For each super effective move on that Pokemon, 67% chance to hard switch to it.
Never in Doubles.

Perish Song:

If the AI's Perish count is about to hit 0:
It will hard switch 100% of the time, unless it has Soundproof.

Yawn:

If the AI is drowsy from Yawn and its HP is above 33%:
It will hard switch 100% of the time.
It will NOT switch if any of these are true:
It already picked a move that KOs you, and it is faster.
It knows Sleep Talk.
It has Marvel Scale, or holds a Wiki Berry, or knows Nature Power and you aren't immune to Snore.
(Those 3 are game bugs. They were meant to be Natural Cure, Shed Skin, Early Bird, Chesto Berry, Lum Berry and Snore.)
It is on the ground and knows Misty Terrain or Electric Terrain.
Its Evasion is +4 or higher, and you don't have Unaware or Keen Eye, and it hasn't been hit by Foresight or Miracle Eye.
It can't fall asleep anyway.

Toxic, Curse, Nightmare, Leech Seed:

If the AI sees:
Badly poisoned for 2+ turns and HP is 33% or more:hard switch 50% of the time
Cursed:hard switch 50% of the time
Nightmare:hard switch 33% of the time
Leech Seed:hard switch 25% of the time
If the AI has any raised stat, these are much lower:
Toxic 10%, Curse 10%, Nightmare 7%, Leech Seed 5%.
It will NOT switch for these if:
It has Magic Guard.
It already picked a move that KOs you, and it is faster.

Infatuation:

If the AI is infatuated:
It will hard switch 100% of the time, unless it already picked a move that KOs you and it is faster.

You are in the air or underground:

If you used Fly, Dig, Dive, Bounce or similar and are off screen:
If the AI has a Pokemon that absorbs the move you last hit it with, it switches to it.
It stays in if it has a raised stat and you can't KO it.
It stays in if it already picked a move that KOs you, it is slower, and you can't KO it.

Absorb switch:

If the last move you landed on the AI was a damaging Fire, Water, Electric or Grass move,
and a back Pokemon has one of these abilities:
Fire:Flash Fire
Water: Water Absorb, Storm Drain, Dry Skin
Electric: Volt Absorb, Motor Drive, Lightning Rod
Grass:Sap Sipper
The AI will hard switch to it:
50% of the time if the AI has no super effective move on you.
17% of the time if the AI has a super effective move on you.
If 2 back Pokemon have the ability, each one gets its own 50% roll, first in party order goes first.
It won't do this if its current Pokemon already has one of those abilities.
This one skips the "Which Pokemon comes in" list.

Natural Cure and Regenerator:

Natural Cure:
Asleep, frozen or badly poisoned: hard switch 100% of the time.
Any other status and HP is 50% or more: hard switch 25% of the time (5% if a stat is raised).
Regenerator:
No status and HP is 67% or lower: hard switch 50% of the time (10% if a stat is raised).
In both cases a back Pokemon has to pass the "Which Pokemon comes in" list below.

Immune to or resists your last move:

The game has a check for this, but it is broken by a bug.
What it's meant to do:
A back Pokemon is immune to your last damaging move and has a super effective move on you:50%
A back Pokemon resists your last damaging move and has a super effective move on you:33%
What really happens:
It never reads which Pokemon it is looking at, so it checks junk data.
In testing it never fired (0 out of 96 tries, across 4 different matchups).
Don't expect this switch. Those Pokemon can still come in through the other checks.
The AI also skips this check if it has a super effective move on you (90%, per move),
or has +4 or more total stat boosts.

Which Pokemon comes in on a hard switch:

Baton Pass comes first:
If a back Pokemon knows Baton Pass and 2 Pokemon are able to come in: it always picks the Baton Pass user.
If 3 or more are able to come in: 33% chance to pick the Baton Pass user.
Otherwise each back Pokemon is ranked. Best first:
1. Faster, OHKOs you, doesn't get 3HKO'd
2. Faster, OHKOs you, doesn't get 2HKO'd
3. Faster, 2HKOs you, doesn't get 3HKO'd
4. Slower, OHKOs you, doesn't get 3HKO'd
5. Faster, OHKOs you, doesn't get OHKO'd
6. Slower, OHKOs you, doesn't get 2HKO'd
7. Slower, 2HKOs you, doesn't get 3HKO'd
8. Slower, 3HKOs you, doesn't get 3HKO'd
A Pokemon that isn't on this list can't come in.
Ties go to the first one in party order.
If nobody is on the list, the AI does NOT switch and just uses a move.
A Pokemon gets thrown off the list if:
It is slower and gets 2HKO'd.
One of your likely moves OHKOs it (100% of the time).
Any of your other moves OHKOs it (50% of the time). This is why you can't always predict it.
Your likely moves are:
Your strongest move against the AI's current Pokemon.
Any move that would KO the AI's current Pokemon.
Any other move, unless another move does 25% more than it, or another move KOs,
or it is resisted and does under half of the AI's current HP.
"Faster" here means truly faster. A speed tie counts as slower.
A Truant Pokemon can't come in while it would be in danger from your Protect or a two-turn move.

Which Pokemon comes in after a KO:

Baton Pass comes first, same as above.
Otherwise each back Pokemon is ranked. Best first:
1. Faster, OHKOs you, doesn't get OHKO'd
2. Faster, OHKOs you
3. Slower, OHKOs you, doesn't get OHKO'd
4. Faster, and wins the damage race
5. Slower, and still wins the damage race after taking a hit first
6. Faster
Ties go to the first one in party order.
If nobody is on this list, the AI sends out the LAST Pokemon still alive in party order.
The damage race works on current HP.
If its best move does a bigger % of your HP than your best move does of its HP, it wins.
Rare bug: if the AI ran its hard switch check earlier that same turn, a Pokemon that isn't on
this list keeps its score from that check, so it can get picked anyway.

Doubles:

Bad matchup and Only bad moves don't exist in Doubles.
All the other checks still run, but they rarely all line up, so switching in Doubles is rare.

All damaging moves:

Highest damaging move:
The AI compares the damage of its attacks and the highest one gets:
+2
Damage above 120% of your HP counts as 120%, so two moves that both easily kill are tied.
Ties go to the move with no recoil, no recharge, or no contact into Rocky Helmet / Iron Barbs / Rough Skin.
After that, ties go to the move with a side effect. If still tied, it's random.
Only one move can get this +2. A second kill move does not get it.
If a damaging move kills:
AI is faster, or the move has priority:
+4
AI is slower:
+2
The move has priority:
Additional +4
(All kills also get the +2 below in "Target's HP".)
Explosion moves never get these kill bonuses.
If a damaging move does not kill:

4x super effective:+4
8x super effective:+8
High crit chance move:+2
Moves in the list below:-1
High crit chance move that is super effective:
Additional +1 (50%)
If AI is slower and you can kill it this turn:
Highest damaging move:+1
Other damaging moves:-1
If the AI can kill you, but it is slower, and you aren't asleep or frozen:
All moves without priority:-1
Moves with priority:+1
A damaging move is "weak" if it is not close to the highest damaging move:
-1
Close means within 30% of your HP, using the same 120% limit.
It is also weak if the highest move is the better pick, even when the damage is close.
For example, the highest move has a side effect and this one is a plain attack.
